About Us

Three decades, unwavering

From drawing near to stepping in, we have cultivated this Montessori garden for more than thirty years — unwavering, and true to where we began.

We believe every child arrives in this world with their own unique rhythm. Our role is to understand the child, to accompany, and to guide — helping each one develop their inherent potential.

Follow the child — this has remained our steadfast belief throughout these thirty years.

Our Faculty

A fully certified Montessori faculty

With patience and respect, we guide each child's development. We encourage continued study, sustaining the momentum of professional growth.
