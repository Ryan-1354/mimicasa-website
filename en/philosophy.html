Our Commitment

Six things we've cared about for many years

The spirit of Montessori education lives not in slogans, but in every carefully considered detail.

From the air to the faculty, from aesthetics to every corner — these are the things we've devoted ourselves to for many years.

01

A Healthy Learning Space

From building materials to air, aroma, and drinking water, we safeguard every unseen detail for the children. Health is the foundation on which learning can happen.

  • Awarded the Taipei City Environmental Protection Bureau's highest Gold Medal honor for air quality
  • Fully non-toxic materials, returning to a pure essence
  • Organic aromas attuned to the four seasons
  • Purified, energized drinking water
  • Technological anti-haze screens that protect air quality

02

A Steady, Experienced Faculty

We encourage and reward teachers' wellbeing and professional development.

  • A fully certified Montessori faculty across the school
  • Consultants visit classrooms regularly to deepen teaching practice
  • Guiding each child's development with respect and patience
  • Rewarding continued study to sustain professional growth

03

A Richly Aesthetic Environment

Beauty is not decoration; it is how a child perceives the world, day after day. Our choices of color, light, material, and object are all forms of education.

  • Rooted in the humanities, shaping a beauty of perception
  • Warm tones and design that create a calm learning atmosphere
  • Every detail and corner reflects the value of aesthetics and education

04

Montessori Cultural Studies, Brought to Life

From working with indoor materials, learning extends out to the school yard, the street corner, and beyond. We let each child's knowledge take firm root in everyday life.

  • Extending the learning setting from indoor materials out into the campus
  • Animals, plants, and geology woven into the yard
  • From observation to hands-on work, knowledge connects gracefully with daily life

05

A Living Montessori Nature Corner

An observation table that changes with the seasons, the 24 solar terms, and the care of small creatures — through caretaking, children learn perception, compassion, and responsibility.

  • A rich and colorful nature observation table
  • Elements that reflect the changing 24 solar terms
  • Caring for small creatures, understanding life through nurture
  • Cultivating respectful perception, compassion, and a sense of responsibility

06

Thoughtfully Crafted Montessori Corners

Every corner has something it means to teach the child.

  • In a calm, orderly, and easeful environment, cultivating focus, independence, confidence, and inner balance
  • A cloakroom, reading nook, flower-arranging area, small kitchen, peace table, and geographic artifacts, all warmly presented
